Basic Course Information, MATH 2001 Fall 2017

University of Georgia · Dr. Beckmann

Course focus

Geometry and measurement for prospective elementary teachers, including angles, shapes, area, perimeter, the Pythagorean theorem, solid geometry, volume, surface area, nets and similarity.

Course work

  • Interactive class activities and regular attendance.
  • Reading and practice exercises.
  • Writing assignments posted through UGA eLC.
  • Take-home tests and group quizzes.
  • Comprehensive final examination.

Academic policies

Students were expected to follow University of Georgia academic-honesty requirements and course instructions.
