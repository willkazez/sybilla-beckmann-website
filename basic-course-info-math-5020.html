Basic Course Information, MATH 5020/7020 Fall 2018

University of Georgia · Dr. Beckmann

Required text

Mathematics for Elementary Teachers with Activities, fifth edition, by Sybilla Beckmann, Pearson. Electronic course ID: beckmann94198.

Course topics

Base-ten numbers; fractions; addition and subtraction; multiplication; multiplication of fractions, decimals and negative numbers; and division.

Objectives

Strengthen understanding of arithmetic, connect arithmetic to algebra, reason about quantities, explain procedures, communicate clearly, explore mathematical phenomena, and use multiple solution methods.

Class work

Attendance and active participation were required. The section was part of the Writing Intensive Program, with writing assignments posted in eLC. MATH 7020 students had additional work such as a project or paper.
