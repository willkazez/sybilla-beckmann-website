Daily Organizer MATH 2001 Fall 2017

Course sequence

Weeks 1–3 — Angles

  • What geometry is and why angles are useful.
  • Angles as rays/space and as amounts of turning; Common Core Grade 4 angle definition.
  • Opposite angles, triangle-angle relationships, parallel lines, adding lines, and angle problem solving.
  • Eratosthenes' method for determining the circumference of the Earth.

Weeks 4–6 — Shapes

  • Mathematical definitions of circles and 2-D shapes.
  • Properties, categories and hierarchies of squares, rectangles, rhombi, parallelograms and trapezoids.
  • Constructing shapes with compass and straightedge, folding/cutting, and walking/turning.

Weeks 7–10 — Measurement and area

  • Measurable attributes and concepts of length and area.
  • Rectangle area, moving and additivity principles.
  • Triangle, parallelogram and trapezoid area formulas and why they work.
  • Circumference and diameter; circle area; irregular-shape area.

Weeks 11–14 — Perimeter, Pythagorean theorem and solids

  • Perimeter versus area and “tilted squares.”
  • Pythagorean theorem and why it is true.
  • Prisms, cylinders, pyramids and cones; volume versus surface area.
  • Patterns/nets and surface-area problem solving.

Week 15 — Similarity

Similarity in mathematics versus everyday language and reasoning with similar shapes. GeoGebra scaling sketch.
