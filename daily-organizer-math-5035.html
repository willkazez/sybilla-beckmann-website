Daily Organizer MATH 5035/7035 Spring 2019

Weeks 1–3 — Fraction division

Two meanings of division, fraction-division word problems, math drawings, common denominators, reciprocal reasoning, and distinguishing division from multiplication.

Weeks 4–7 — Proportional relationships

Multiple-batches and variable-parts perspectives; double number lines; strip diagrams; ratios as rates; proportional relationships; equations of lines; inverse relationships. GeoGebra and additional archived sketches are included.

Weeks 8–12 — Statistics and probability

Proportional versus inverse relationships; statistical problem solving; mean, median and mode; variation; comparing distributions; probability of compound events.

Weeks 13–14 — Number theory

Factors and multiples; prime numbers; Sieve of Eratosthenes; trial division; factor trees; Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic; GCF and LCM.

Weeks 15–16 — Review and final

Review of fraction division and proportional reasoning; final exam May 2, noon–3 p.m. The archive also links to the NCTM adjustable spinner.
