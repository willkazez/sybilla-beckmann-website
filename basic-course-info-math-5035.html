Basic Course Information, MATH 5035/7035 Spring 2019

University of Georgia · Dr. Beckmann

Required text

Mathematics for Elementary Teachers with Activities, fifth edition, by Sybilla Beckmann, Pearson.

Course topics

Fraction division; ratio and proportional relationships; statistics including random samples, measures of center and variation, and comparing distributions; probability including theoretical and experimental probability and compound events; and, if time permitted, number theory.

Objectives

Strengthen understanding of fraction arithmetic, algebra, statistics and probability, and number theory; explain why procedures work; communicate mathematically; explore mathematical phenomena; and use multiple solution methods.

Class work and assignments

Attendance was required because interactive class work was central. Students completed daily reading and writing assignments, explained solutions precisely, and revised work when requested.

Grading

  • Tests and quizzes — 55%
  • Assignments — 15%
  • Final exam — 30%

MATH 7035 students completed an additional course project. UGA academic-honesty requirements applied.
