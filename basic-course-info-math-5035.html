Basic Course Information, MATH 5035/7035 Spring 2019

University of Georgia · Dr. Beckmann

Required text

Mathematics for Elementary Teachers with Activities, fifth edition, by Sybilla Beckmann, Pearson.

Course topics

Fraction division; ratio and proportional relationships; statistics; probability; and, if time permitted, number theory.

Objectives

Strengthen understanding of fraction arithmetic, algebra, statistics, probability and number theory; explain why procedures work; communicate mathematically; explore mathematical phenomena; and use multiple solution methods.

Grading

  • Tests and quizzes — 55%
  • Assignments — 15%
  • Final exam — 30%

MATH 7035 students completed an additional project. UGA academic-honesty requirements applied.
