Dr. Sybilla Beckmann

Sybilla Beckmann is a Josiah Meigs Distinguished Professor of Mathematics, Emeritus, at the University of Georgia. She is interested in the mathematical education of teachers and is author of Mathematics for Elementary and Middle School Teachers with Activities, published by Pearson.
