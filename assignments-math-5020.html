Assignments and announcements, MATH 5020/7020 Fall 2018

Weeks 1–4 — Base ten and fractions

Fraction survey; Sections 1.1, 1.2 and 2.2; Common Core definition of fraction; partitioning and equivalent fractions.

Weeks 5–6 — Comparing fractions and addition/subtraction

Fraction comparison, percent, take-home test, Section 2.5, and fraction addition/subtraction word problems.

Weeks 7–10 — Multiplication

Definition and properties of multiplication; whole-number algorithm; distributive reasoning; GeoGebra activity; UGA faculty handout; take-home test.

Weeks 11–15 — Fraction multiplication and division

Fraction multiplication, division, standard division algorithm, quotients and remainders, review, and MATH 7020 project.

Week 16

Final exam December 6, 8–11 a.m., in 229 Aderhold.
