Assignments and announcements, MATH 5020/7020 Fall 2018

Weeks 1–4 — Base ten and fractions

August 14–September 6: course information; fraction survey; Sections 1.1, 1.2 and 2.2; Common Core definition of fraction; partitioning and equivalent fractions; math drawings.

Weeks 5–6 — Comparing fractions and addition/subtraction

September 11–20: fraction comparison, percent, take-home test, Section 2.5, fraction addition/subtraction word problems and the role of the referent/unit amount.

Weeks 7–10 — Multiplication

September 25–October 18: definition of multiplication; properties; cupcake combinations; whole-number multiplication algorithm; distributive reasoning; GeoGebra activity; UGA faculty handout; take-home test.

Weeks 11–12 — Fraction multiplication

October 23–November 1: meaning of multiplication with fractions, word problems and math drawings.

Weeks 13–15 — Division

November 6–29: division; connection between fraction multiplication and division; whole-number division algorithm; quotients and remainders; review; MATH 7020 project.

Week 16

December 4 had no class; December 5 was reading day; final exam December 6, 8–11 a.m., in 229 Aderhold.
