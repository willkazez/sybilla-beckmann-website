Assignments and announcements, MATH 2001 Fall 2017

Assignments were posted shortly after class. The archived course covered geometry, measurement, area, volume, the Pythagorean theorem, and similarity.

Weekly archive

Weeks 1–3 — Angles

August 15–31: course information; Chapter 10 and Section 10.1; angle relationships; opposite angles; triangle-angle reasoning; Class Activities 10B, 10C, 10G and 10W; Robot Robby's turning-path problem.

Weeks 4–6 — Shapes

September 5–21: circles; defining and classifying triangles, quadrilaterals and polygons; Sections 10.3–10.4; take-home test; category relationships; compass-and-straightedge constructions.

Weeks 7–10 — Measurement and area

September 26–October 19: measurement; length and area; Sections 11.1, 12.1, 12.2–12.4 and 12.6; area of rectangles, triangles and parallelograms; take-home test.

Weeks 11–14 — Area, Pythagorean theorem and solids

October 24–November 16: perimeter versus area; area of circles; group quizzes; Pythagorean theorem; prisms, cylinders, pyramids and cones; volume, surface area and nets.

Weeks 15–16 — Similarity and final exam

November 28–December 7: similarity review, reading day, and the comprehensive final exam on December 7 from noon–3 p.m. in 229 Aderhold.
