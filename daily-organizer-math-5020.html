Daily Organizer MATH 5020/7020 Fall 2018

Course sequence

Weeks 1–5 — Base ten, fractions and percent

Base-ten structure; Common Core fractions; fraction reasoning and partitioning; equivalent fractions; comparing fractions; percentages; math drawings and unit amounts.

Weeks 6–10 — Addition, subtraction and multiplication

Why fraction addition and subtraction work; word problems; the definition of multiplication; multiplication properties; whole-number algorithms; distributive reasoning; multiplication of fractions. The archive includes GeoGebra sketches and UGA faculty resources.

Weeks 11–15 — Fraction multiplication and division

Reasoning about fraction multiplication; division; interpreting quotients; explaining the standard division algorithm using base-ten bundles and equal groups; review.

Week 16

Final review and the December 6 final exam.

GeoGebra and archived UGA faculty resources are preserved.
