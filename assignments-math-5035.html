Assignments and Announcements, MATH 5035/7035 Spring 2019

Weeks 1–3 — Fraction division

Sections 6.4–6.5; two meanings of division; math drawings; common denominators; reciprocal reasoning.

Weeks 4–7 — Ratio and proportional relationships

Multiple-batches and variable-parts perspectives; double number lines; strip diagrams; proportional relationships and equations.

Weeks 8–12 — Statistics and probability

Proportional versus inverse relationships; statistical questions; data collection and analysis; measures of center and variation; probability.

Weeks 13–14 — Number theory

Factors, multiples, primes, Sieve of Eratosthenes, trial division, factor trees, GCF and LCM.

Weeks 15–16 — Review and final

Review and final exam May 2, noon–3 p.m. MATH 7035 students also completed a course project.
