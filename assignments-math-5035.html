Assignments and Announcements, MATH 5035/7035 Spring 2019

Weeks 1–3 — Fraction division

January 10–24: Sections 6.4–6.5; two meanings of division; math drawings; common denominators; reciprocal reasoning; distinguishing division from multiplication situations.

Weeks 4–7 — Ratio and proportional relationships

January 29–February 21: multiple-batches and variable-parts perspectives; double number lines and strip diagrams; proportional relationships and equations; GeoGebra resources.

Weeks 8–12 — Statistics and probability

February 26 onward: proportional versus inverse relationships; statistical questions; data collection and analysis; measures of center and variation; probability of compound events.

Weeks 13–14 — Probability and number theory

April 9–18: multi-stage experiments; independence/dependence; factors and multiples; prime numbers; Sieve of Eratosthenes; trial division; factor trees; Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic; GCF and LCM.

Weeks 15–16 — Review and final

April 23–May 2: review of fraction division and proportional reasoning; final exam May 2, noon–3 p.m. MATH 7035 students also completed a course project.
